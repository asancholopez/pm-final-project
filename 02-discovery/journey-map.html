Future-State Journey: One Route, One Record

From paper and WhatsApp backups to an app drivers trust from first stop to last

Persona: Experienced drivers on long routes (40+ stops) using Android phones.
Goal: Finish every stop quickly and leave a delivery record they don't have to double-check.
Friction: Core actions are slow and unreliable.
Today's workaround: paper manifest as backup → text the dispatcher instead of “Mark delivered” → retake proof-of-delivery photos up to 3× → after a crash, phone the office (~20 min) → finish the route off-app.
1

Start the Day

User Action
Taps “Start Route” on home screen → route begins in one tap.
Internal State
Whole route saved on phone → leaves the paper manifest behind.
Pain Point Addressed
“Start Route” buried 2–3 levels deep → now first thing on screen.
2

Drive the Route

User Action
Drives past stop 40 without interruption → no stops lost.
Internal State
Stops bracing for a crash → focuses on driving, not backups.
Pain Point Addressed
Android crash past ~40 stops → no 20-minute call to the office.
3

Complete the Stop

User Action
Taps “Mark delivered” once; time, location, photo attach → logged in seconds.
Internal State
Sees “Saved, uploading when signal returns” → no retakes, no doubt.
Pain Point Addressed
Three screens and silent photo failures → one tap, confirmed record.
4

Close the Route

User Action
Completes last stop → day's record already complete, nothing to reconcile.
Internal State
Heads home without texting dispatch → work is visible and done.
Pain Point Addressed
Texts to dispatch and double entry on paper → app is the single record.

Competitive Advantages over the Manual Workaround

  1. Recorded once, not twice → no paper and chat duplication at every stop.
  2. Dispatch sees status live → no texts, calls or 20-minute crash recoveries.
  3. Confirmed proof of delivery → a record customers and reporting can trust.
Future state is a design hypothesis, not validated. Pain points come from 12 research sessions and 10 open bug reports (02-discovery/ai-synthesis.md). Stage 4 and Advantage 2 also depend on fixing the 20–60 min dispatcher dashboard lag. Stage 3's auto-captured location depends on the known GPS drift (up to 200 m).
