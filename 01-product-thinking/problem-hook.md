# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** The company will lose customers to competitors offering streamlined solutions that are easy to adopt and use.

The core value of the logistics platform lies in the speedy expedition and coordination of operations. The more complex it becomes to handle, the less value it delivers. This will cause customers considering simplified alternatives to move to competitors.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The frontline operators need to resolve things quickly and move on. 

Anything that slows them down will push them to find a workaround outside of the platform.  

At end of shift, a dispatcher closing [N] deliveries skips the cost and invoice fields because each takes [N] extra screens, then re-enters them in a Google Sheet that finance reconciles by hand.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop mid-market fleets from churning to leaner tools by removing the compliance steps that slow frontline operators' daily dispatch work
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For frontline fleet operators, we will restore fast execution on core coordination tasks (eg. assigning loads, closing deliveries) while keeping enterprise compliance out of sight because operational slowdown is driving daily users off the platform and putting core revenue at risk of churning to leaner tools.
