# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** The company will lose customers to competitors offering streamlined solutions that are easy to adopt and use.

The core value of the logistics platform lies in the speedy expedition and coordination of operations. The more complex it becomes to handle, the less value it delivers. This will cause customers considering simplified alternatives to move to competitors.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** At end of shift, a driver closing [N] deliveries skips the compliance checks (including cost and invoice fields) because each takes [N] extra screens, then re-enters them in a Google Sheet that finance reconciles by hand.

The drivers need to resolve things quickly and move on. 

Anything that slows them down will push them to find a workaround outside of the platform.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must stop mid-market fleets from churning to leaner tools by removing the unnecessary steps that slow fleet drivers daily dispatch work
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For drivers, we will restore fast execution on core tasks (eg. closing deliveries) while keeping enterprise compliance lightweight because operational slowdown is driving daily users off the platform and putting core revenue at risk of churning to leaner tools.

## Finalized hypothesis _(complete in Module 3)_

> Based on drivers re-entering skipped compliance and cost fields into a Google Sheet at end of shift, and a funnel drop from 71% to 48% at the compliance step (11.3 min vs. a 3.0 min benchmark), we believe that cutting the compliance close-out flow to a single screen for drivers will raise in-app compliance completion from 48% to 60%+ and reduce median completion time from 11.3 to ≤5 min. We will protect compliance record accuracy and core dispatch-flow speed, and make a go/no-go decision after one quarter (checkpoint at week 6).
